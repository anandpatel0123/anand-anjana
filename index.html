24 June 2026

Anand & You

Everything from the days we became us, kept in one place, just for you.

My dearest wife,

I wanted to give you something that won’t get lost in a phone, so I made you this.

On 24 June 2026 we walked a path with seven promises written along it. There was fog at our feet, fire on both sides, and what felt like every person we have ever met holding up a camera. I remember almost none of that. I remember you.

Some of what’s here you’ve seen a hundred times. Some of it you’ve never seen at all: the turmeric, the sunglasses, the moment I bowed at our mandir before leaving to come and get you. That was my side of the story, and all of it was on its way to you.

I also saw the morning you said goodbye to your home. I know what that asked of you. Whatever I do with the rest of my life, I want it to be worth that morning.

Keep this. Come back to it on the days I’ve annoyed you. Thank you for being my wife.

Yours, always,Anand

The Ring Ceremony

Lagun

The Lagun came with trays of gifts and a patrika that fixed every date in ink. After that there was no pretending. The countdown had started, and I sat there garlanded and blessed, thinking about you.

24 June 2026

The Wedding

The day all of it was for.

Coming to get you

I bowed at our mandir, took the blessings at the door, and then we set off with lights, paper in the air, and more dancing than anyone had planned for.

Then you arrived

You came in under a canopy of flowers. I can’t tell you what was happening around me, because I wasn’t looking at anything else.

The path of seven promises

Our path was marked with seven signs. We walked it together through the fog and the sparks, your hand in mine, one promise at a time.

Read the promises

Varmala

Two garlands, two very excited families, and one twirl for the cameras. Then everyone we love came up on stage, one group at a time, to bless us.

The morning

By morning the lights were off. It was only the fire, the sindoor, and your hands with mine. All that noise, and this quiet part was what made us married.

Vidai

I know what this morning was for you. I was standing right there. You left one home for me, and I have not forgotten it for a single day since.
